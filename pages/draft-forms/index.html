Template Defaults

Set the standing answers your firm wants on every generated form (for example, the I-821D Part 4 questions that are always "No"). Download a template, check boxes and fill anything that never changes in your PDF viewer, save it, then upload it back — those answers will appear on every draft from then on. Client-specific fields stay autofilled from the client card and are ignored if edited here. Re-uploading replaces the previous defaults.
