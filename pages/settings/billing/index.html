Clicking Connect opens FreshBooks' sign-in page. Sign in as the firm's FreshBooks owner and click Allow to grant access. You'll be returned here automatically.

Once connected, sending an invoice from a matter creates it in FreshBooks (matched to the client by email address), and unbilled time is pulled from FreshBooks time tracking. Access can be revoked anytime with Disconnect.

FreshBooks isn't configured on this deployment yet. The OAuth app credentials (FRESHBOOKS_CLIENT_ID, FRESHBOOKS_CLIENT_SECRET, FRESHBOOKS_REDIRECT_URI) and BILLING_PROVIDER=freshbooks must be set on the worker first.

Billing workflow

Where invoices are written
Choose the one workflow this firm uses. The Billing page shows that entry point only — sending, resending, voiding and payment tracking work the same either way.
Collect into the trust account
Payment links for invoices and retainers route to the firm's trust/IOLTA account by default. Turn this off only if the firm bills from an operating account and its fee agreements and jurisdiction allow it — unearned client funds generally must be held in trust.

Billing increment

Minimum time increment
Billable time rounds up to this increment when pulled for invoicing. The actual tracked time is always shown next to the rounded value so you can review every adjustment before it reaches an invoice.

Retainer prepayments

Auto-record retainers in FreshBooks
When a retainer is paid, also record it in FreshBooks as a Prepayment credit under the client, ready to apply against their next invoice. While off, the retainer-paid email reminds the team to record it in FreshBooks manually.

Leave this off until the automatic recording has been verified against the firm's live FreshBooks account.
