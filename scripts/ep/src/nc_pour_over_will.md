## Article V: Residue (Pour-Over to Trust)

I give the residue of my estate, real and personal, wherever located, including lapsed gifts but excluding property over which I hold a power of appointment, to the Trustee then serving under **The {{principal_name}} Revocable Living Trust dated __________**, signed before this Will, as it may be amended before my death. The Trustee shall add this property to the trust and hold and distribute it under the trust's terms, as written at my death.

If this gift to the trust is ineffective for any reason, I give the residue to the Trustee named in that trust Agreement, to be held on the terms of that Agreement as in effect on the date of this Will, which I incorporate by reference under N.C.G.S. § 31-47.
